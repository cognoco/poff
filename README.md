# Poff! — a free breathing break

![Poff app icon](assets/poff-icon.png)

Poff is a free guided breathing app for short breaks. Choose a one- to fifteen-minute session, follow the sound or animation, and return to your day. No account, ads or subscription.

**[Try Poff in your browser](https://poff.app/breathe)** · **[Get the mobile app](https://poff.app/get)** · **[Official website](https://poff.app/)**

## What Poff offers

- One-, three-, five-, ten- and fifteen-minute breathing sessions.
- Visual pacing and sound cues. The iPhone and Android apps also offer vibration and bundled spoken guidance.
- Offline breathing sessions in the downloaded mobile apps, including airplane mode.
- No account, ads, subscription, score or streak to maintain.
- Optional tips that unlock no features.

For an eyes-closed break, choose Relax and sit somewhere comfortable and safe. Movement modes require eyes open and appropriate space. The mobile and browser versions can differ; the offline promise applies to the downloaded mobile apps.

## Common questions

**Does it work offline?** Yes, the downloaded iPhone and Android apps run breathing sessions with their bundled sounds, speech and vibration without internet. Downloads, updates and optional tips need a connection. Browser offline availability is not guaranteed. [Offline guide](https://poff.app/offline-breathing-app).

**Can I use it between meetings?** Yes. Choose Relax, set one or three minutes in Settings, and press Start. Skip the opening introduction if you want the timed session to begin immediately. [Short-break guide](https://poff.app/breathing-break).

**Do I need an account or subscription?** No. Breathing features are free, and optional tips do not unlock anything.

**Is it a treatment or a quitting-smoking programme?** No. Poff is for everyday relaxation and general wellness. “A smoke break. Without the smoke.” describes a break ritual that anyone can use. Poff does not diagnose, treat or cure a medical condition.
